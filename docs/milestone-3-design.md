# Milestone 3 implementation design

The user authorized M3 after M2 was delivered. The master specification remains unchanged. No M4 stock or asset workflow is included.

## Boundaries and decisions

- Extend the existing Next.js feature services and reviewed PostgreSQL migrations. All routes retain owner authentication, CSRF, origin validation, strict schemas and backup locking.
- Quote drafts contain manual labour/services/products; stock reservation remains M4. Publishing freezes the agreed parties, amounts, taxes, terms and PDF. A published quote may be manually shared without claiming SMTP acceptance. Explicit acceptance/decline records the owner's evidence; expiry is derived from the business date. One accepted quote can create at most one linked job and one active invoice. Discarded drafts and void invoices retain their quote links; a replacement can be created with history preserved. Conversion copies the frozen prices and tax snapshots, regardless of later settings; draft edits must disclose departures from the accepted quote.
- Reuse the print template for quotes with expiry and no invoice payment balance. Keep original PDFs unchanged. Quote numbers use their own locked counter. Published quote corrections require a new draft revision, with predecessor history preserved.
- Tax presets are explicitly configured by the owner, never inferred from location or registration. Support separate tax components and optional compounding, zero-rated and exempt treatment. Snapshot effective components and cents per line; later preset edits do not reprice saved drafts until explicitly reapplied. M2 manual tax inputs remain compatible.
- Expenses track actual vendor cost/tax/payment and optional client/job/context notes/receipt. Customer recovery amount is separate from cost. Trips track manual distance, purpose, optional locations, labour duration and independently chosen mileage/time rates; no assumed CRA deduction or rate. Full immutable customer charges enter an unbilled queue and are reserved once per draft/invoice. No partial source allocation. Voids release reservations; ordinary credits do not silently unbill sources.
- Credits/refunds are append-only invoice adjustments, separate from original totals and receipts. Net customer obligation = issued total less credits; net cash = receipts less receipt reversals less refunds plus refund reversals. Outstanding and customer-credit balance are displayed separately, never silently negative. Refunds cannot exceed the customer credit or net receipts. These events record external money movement; the app does not initiate bank transfers. Reversals carry a reason and reference the original event.
- SMTP uses a pinned maintained Nodemailer SMTP transport, not a selectable arbitrary transport or URL/file attachment resolver. Optional deployment `SMTP_ENCRYPTION_KEY` is a 32-byte dedicated key; AES-256-GCM versioned settings envelopes bind the singleton/purpose as authenticated data. Missing/wrong key fails closed; no SESSION_SECRET or client-vault key fallback. Preserve the deployment key outside backups. TLS modes are verified implicit TLS/required STARTTLS; plain transport is an explicit local-relay choice with clear warning and cannot carry authenticated SMTP credentials.
- Persist email attempt metadata and exact document hash before sending. Record server acceptance only after one explicit recipient is accepted; do not claim inbox delivery. Auth/rejection errors are failed, ambiguous transport failures are unknown. Stable idempotency prevents automatic resends; unknown/pending attempts require explicit owner review and a new attempt. Never log raw SMTP exceptions, passwords or message bodies. Testing uses disposable local SMTP fixtures, never real recipients.

## Data relationships

`quote -> client`, immutable quote events and frozen document snapshots; optional predecessor quote. `invoice.source_quote_id` is unique among active invoices; `job.source_quote_id` is unique. Historical discarded/void invoices retain their links. `expense -> optional client/job`, contextual notes and private receipt attachments. `trip -> optional client/job`. `client_charge -> expense OR trip`, `charge_allocation -> charge/invoice/line` with one active full allocation. `invoice_adjustment -> invoice`, optional self-reference for reversal. `tax_profile` stores versioned components applied as snapshots. Singleton `smtp_settings` stores encrypted password only. `email_attempt -> invoice OR quote`, and append-only `email_result -> attempt` records the outcome.

## Verification gates

Preserve all M0–M2 tests. Add real PostgreSQL races and direct-SQL invariants for quote conversion, full charge allocation and credit/refund bounds; exact tax/mileage arithmetic; successful and rejected/ambiguous SMTP fixture sends; plaintext SMTP-secret absence and missing/wrong-key behavior. Browser flows cover quote editing/publishing/acceptance/conversion, expense/receipt/trip billing, credits/refunds and settings in both themes and ordinary HTTP while vault restrictions remain unchanged. Render and inspect Letter/multipage A4 quote PDFs. Extend fictional seed and Docker backup/fresh-restore assertions for new relationships, encrypted settings and immutable documents. Run CI before pushing main; handoff and stop for M4 confirmation.
